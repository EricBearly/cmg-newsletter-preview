Toyota tops CBB Best Retained Value Awards for 10th straight year, sweeps 3 categories
Porsche led the premium category and Kia led electric vehicles in Canadian Black Book’s 2026 awards
Canadian Black Book named Toyota the winner in the mainstream category of its 2026 Best Retained Value Awards, with Porsche top of the premium category and Kia leading electric vehicles. Toyota has now been the top mainstream brand for retained value for 10 consecutive years, and took first, second and third place in three categories: Car: Mainstream, SUV: Mainstream Sub-Compact/Compact and SUV: Mainstream Mid/Full-size.

Wholesale vehicle price declines nearly triple last week
Canadian wholesale vehicle values fell almost three times as fast last week, with average depreciation rising from 0.12% to 0.35%, according to Canadian Black Book’s latest Market Insights report. The average decline for the same week across 2017 to 2019 was 0.19%.
