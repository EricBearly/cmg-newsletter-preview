Copart’s acquisition of ACV could help ‘make a splash’ in commercial consignment, too
The $1.9 billion all-cash deal closed last week, opening a route further into whole-car remarketing
Copart’s $1.9 billion all-cash acquisition of ACV Auctions, which closed last week, is a chance to push further into whole-car remarketing, particularly among franchise dealers. Chief executive Jay Adair said shortly after the deal was announced, “I think this opens up the world and our ability to do a lot more vehicles in that space.” One analyst sees the commercial consignment market as a second place the combination could make a mark.

NADA elects first female chair since 2008, will be second woman overall in role
Sandy Fitzgerald Angello, a dealer operator at Pohanka Automotive Group in Maryland, was elected by the board to become chair of the National Automobile Dealers Association. She will be the second woman to hold the role and the first in nearly two decades.
